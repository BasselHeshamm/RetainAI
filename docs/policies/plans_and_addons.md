# Plans and Add-ons

*Fictional reference written for the RetainAI demo.*

## Internet plans
- DSL: up to 100 Mbps. Lower price, lower churn historically.
- Fiber optic: up to 1 Gbps. Higher price; fiber customers churn more often, usually citing price or reliability.

## Contracts
- Month-to-month: cancel any time, no early termination fee. Highest churn of all contract types.
- One year: early termination fee of $100.
- Two year: early termination fee of $200. Lowest churn.

## Add-ons (monthly price)
- Online Security: $8
- Online Backup: $7
- Device Protection: $7
- Tech Support: $9
- Streaming TV: $10
- Streaming Movies: $10

Customers with more add-ons tend to stay longer. Security and Tech Support are the add-ons most associated with lower churn.

## Payment methods
Automatic payments (bank transfer or credit card) are associated with lower churn than Electronic check or Mailed check.
