# Discount Approval Policy

*Fictional policy written for the RetainAI demo.*

## Approval levels
- Offers with a total value up to $100 over their lifetime can be applied by any retention agent.
- Offers worth $100 to $300 need a team lead's approval in the CRM before they are applied.
- Offers worth more than $300 need approval from the retention manager, and a short written justification.

## How to calculate offer value
Offer value is the total discount the customer receives over the offer period. Example: 15% off a $70 bill for 12 months is 0.15 × 70 × 12 = $126, which needs team lead approval.

## Prioritisation
Retention budget should go first to customers with High churn risk and Monthly charges above the median, because they represent the most revenue at risk. Revenue at risk for one customer is churn probability × monthly charges × 12.

## What not to offer
- Do not offer a discount to customers with Low churn risk unless they ask to cancel.
- Do not stack more than two offers.
- Never promise a price that is not in the Retention Offer Catalogue.
