# Retention Offer Catalogue

*Fictional policy written for the RetainAI demo. Not a real operator's terms.*

## R1 – Contract Upgrade Discount
Eligibility: customer on a Month-to-month contract with tenure of at least 3 months.
Offer: 15% off the monthly bill for 12 months when the customer moves to a One year contract, or 20% for 12 months when moving to a Two year contract.
Best for: customers whose churn risk is driven mainly by the month-to-month contract.

## R2 – Security & Support Bundle
Eligibility: internet customers (DSL or Fiber optic) without Online Security or Tech Support.
Offer: Online Security and Tech Support free for 6 months, then 50% off for the following 6 months.
Best for: customers whose risk is raised by having no security or support add-ons. Customers with support add-ons churn noticeably less.

## R3 – Fiber Price Match
Eligibility: Fiber optic customers paying more than $85 per month with tenure of at least 6 months.
Offer: a one-time credit of $50 plus a $10 per month reduction for 6 months.
Best for: high-bill fiber customers who mention price.

## R4 – AutoPay Switch Credit
Eligibility: customers paying by Electronic check or Mailed check.
Offer: $5 per month credit for 12 months after switching to Bank transfer (automatic) or Credit card (automatic).
Best for: customers whose risk is raised by manual payment methods. Can be combined with R1 or R2.

## R5 – New Customer Welcome Call
Eligibility: tenure of 6 months or less.
Offer: a proactive onboarding call from a senior agent, plus one free month of a streaming add-on.
Best for: brand-new customers in their first months, when churn risk is highest.

## R6 – Loyalty Reward
Eligibility: tenure of at least 24 months.
Offer: a free device protection add-on for 12 months, or a one-time $30 credit, whichever the customer prefers.
Best for: long-standing customers showing new signs of risk.

## Combining offers
At most two offers may be combined for one customer. R1 can be combined with R2 or R4. R3 cannot be combined with R1.
