# Customer Contact Guidelines for Retention

*Fictional guidelines written for the RetainAI demo.*

## Channel
- High risk and monthly charges above $70: phone call from a retention agent within 3 days.
- High risk, lower bill: personalised SMS or email with the offer, then a call if there is no response in 7 days.
- Medium risk: include the offer in the next monthly bill message.

## Senior citizens
Senior citizens should be contacted by phone rather than SMS, and offered R2 (Security & Support Bundle) first, since tech support is the most valued add-on in this group.

## Contact limits
Do not contact the same customer about retention more than twice in 30 days. Respect opt-outs recorded in the CRM.

## What to say
Lead with what the customer gains, mention the offer period and price after it ends, and never pressure the customer. Record the outcome in the CRM.
